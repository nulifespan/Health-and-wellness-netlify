First off, lets not underestimate the role of our adrenal glands. These small but mighty glands sit atop our kidneys and are responsible for producing hormones like cortisol and adrenaline, which help us respond to stress. So, when life throws curveballs our way, our adrenal glands are the ones that help us handle them. But heres the catch: theyre not invincible.
